# Brief triage (10:00 to 10:45 on day 1)

Score each brief 1 to 5. Highest total wins. Decide by 10:45 (the event repo switch needs the concept), no revisiting after 13:00.

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Demo-ability | x3 | Can we show it working live in under 2 minutes? |
| Fit to our prep | x2 | How much of our prepared specs and handoffs apply? |
| Competition | x2 | Will half the room pick this? Fewer rivals is better |
| Judge appeal | x2 | Does it solve something the sponsor cares about in their own business? |
| Data access | x1 | Can we get the data / APIs in the first hour? |
| Our edge | x1 | Do we know the domain better than most students? |

Then write a one-line pitch: "For <user> who <problem>, we built <thing> that <result>." If that sentence is weak, pick the next brief.
