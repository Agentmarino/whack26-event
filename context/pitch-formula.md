# Pitch formula (5 minutes)

1. Problem in one line, with a real number or example (20s)
2. Live demo of the one flow that matters (2 min)
3. How it works, one diagram (45s)
4. Why it matters to the sponsor's own business (45s)
5. What is next (20s)
6. Spare time for questions

Rules: demo before slides. Have a recorded backup. Name the team clearly.
