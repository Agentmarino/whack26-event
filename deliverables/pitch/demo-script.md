# Demo script: 2 minutes on the iPhone

Before judging: backend warm (one full Priya run), phone on the same network as the laptop (or the hotspot), app open on the greeting, Demo mode ON (instant text, samples auto-picked), Plain English and read aloud OFF (they go on at 0:05 and 1:55), volume up for one read-aloud moment. Backups: `DEMO_OFFLINE=true`, then the recorded video. Numbers in brackets come from the engine at freeze; fill them from `numbers.md`.

| Time | Do | Judges see | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | (agent idle at the desk) | The pixel agent at the Capital One desk, glass office behind | "This is Priya. 23, renting, one card. Capital One just turned her down for a Balance Transfer card, and the letter doesn't tell her why." |
| 0:05 | While you talk: Settings (top right) → Plain English on → close | Settings sheet opens and closes; the greeting stays | (keep going with the 0:00 line; from here the diagnosis and plan come in plain words) |
| 0:10 | Tap "Use a sample" → Priya; bank sample; drop the decline letter | Agent takes each document, reads it | "She hands over her credit report, her bank data and the letter. Uploads work too; for the demo we use her sample files." |
| 0:25 | Tap the chips for the three questions | Goal, time frame, a question the agent built from her data | "He asks what she wants, and one thing he noticed: she moved in August." |
| 0:35 | Next | Headline, then reason 1 | "One idea at a time. First: she's using [79%] of her limit." |
| 0:45 | Tap "Why?" on reason 1 | The because drawer: rule, her data, the source | "Every number has a because. Her own data, the rule, and where the rule comes from." |
| 0:55 | Next, next | Reasons 2 and 3, then the decline card | "A late payment in July, three applications in two months. That's most likely why Capital One said no. The one fix, and the date to check QuickCheck again: [date]." |
| 1:10 | Next | Agent slides the paper across; tap it | "He hands her the summary to keep." |
| 1:20 | Tap "3-month plan" | Week-by-week steps with her amounts and payees, then the projection chart | "A dated plan with her own numbers. Estimated range at three months: [735 to 836], up from [700]." |
| 1:35 | Next | Card ladder | "And the Capital One path: Classic once her searches clear, with Spotify, Netflix and her phone bill on it, paid in full. Each criterion for the Balance Transfer card shows the month it turns green." |
| 1:45 | Type or say "what if I pay 300 a month?" | What-if card, before and after | "She can ask what if, in her own words." |
| 1:55 | Settings: Read replies aloud on → close | He reads his what-if answer aloud | "And it's accessible: plain English, read aloud, voice in, colour-safe modes." |
| 2:00 | End | | "Credit made understandable, one conversation at a time." |
