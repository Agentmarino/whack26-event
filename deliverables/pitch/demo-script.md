# Demo script: about 2 minutes 30 on the iPhone

Before judging: `pnpm dev` from the repo root, phone on the same network as the laptop (or the hotspot), opened on the frontend's `Network:` URL. Backend warm (one full declined walk with Priya). Demo mode ON (instant text, Priya is picked for every sample). Plain English and read aloud OFF (they go on at 0:05 and 2:00). Ring/silent switch off and volume up: iPhone mutes the web voice in silent mode. Backups: `DEMO_OFFLINE=true` (every answer path of the declined branch is cached in Claude's words), then the recorded video.

Words in quotes are what Cap says on this path (Priya, declined branch, Plain English on), from the real backend on 2026-11-07. The plan starts on Monday 9 Nov 2026; re-check the dates and numbers at freeze.

116 game screen: Cap's words come up in the dialogue box at the bottom, a few lines a page. A blinking ▼ means there is more: tap **Next** (or the box) for the next page or line.
